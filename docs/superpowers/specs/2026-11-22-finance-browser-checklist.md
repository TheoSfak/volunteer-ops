# Οικονομικά — browser checklist (v3.510.0)

Run on the local XAMPP site with AI enabled and a Gemini key, as a system admin, then once as a custom-role user with only «Ετήσιες Συνδρομές».

1. Menu: «Οικονομικά» section after «Επιχειρησιακό»; «Ετήσιες Συνδρομές» is in it and no longer under «Διοίκηση». The dues-only user sees only «Ετήσιες Συνδρομές» there; opening finance.php redirects them.
2. Ρυθμίσεις Οικονομικών: add «Τράπεζα» (BANK) with an opening balance; map «Τραπεζική κατάθεση / IRIS» to it. An opening balance such as «abc» or «1.2.3» is refused; `1500.00`, «1.500» and «1.500,00» are accepted (see 18). A duplicate category name is refused.
3. Νέο έξοδο → «Φωτογραφία» on a phone: the camera opens (a real phone-camera scan, not a desktop file). Scan a real fuel receipt: fields fill, the provider name shows, uncertain fields are outlined. The privacy notice «Η εικόνα αποστέλλεται στον πάροχο AI (π.χ. Google) για ανάγνωση.» is visible. Save with «Πληρώθηκε ήδη» from the cash account. The document shows «Εξοφλημένο»; the cash balance drops.
4. Scan a multi-rate supermarket invoice (24% + 13%): two VAT lines; totals match the printed total.
5. Upload a PDF invoice (a real PDF scan): the first page is rendered in the browser and read. Uploading a non-image renamed to .jpg is refused (only real JPEG/PNG/WebP). Turn «Πληρώθηκε ήδη» off and set a due date 3 days ahead: it appears in Υποχρεώσεις in amber. Open the uploaded PDF from the paperclip in Chrome and on a phone: it renders inline (images are served with a sandbox CSP, PDFs are not).
6. Partial payment from the document's «Πληρωμές» box: status «Μερικώς», remaining correct; paying more than the remainder is refused. On a paid document, lowering the total below the amount paid is refused.
7. Type a printed total 0,50 € off the lines: the warning appears and saving needs the checkbox.
8. ΑΦΜ: «EL 123456789» style input (EL prefix, spaces) is accepted when the check digit is valid; a wrong check digit is flagged in the browser and refused by the server.
9. Edit a document whose category has since been deactivated, and one linked to an old (closed) mission: the category / mission link is kept after saving.
10. Κινήσεις: a bank fee without a document; a transfer cash → bank; deleting the transfer removes both halves. With more than 500 rows the notice about the 500-row limit appears.
11. Ετήσιες Συνδρομές: record a 30 € payment by «Τραπεζική κατάθεση»: an IN movement «Συνδρομή — <name>» appears in the bank account. Edit it to 35 €: the movement follows. Delete it: the movement goes. The movement has no delete button in Κινήσεις (dues movements can only be removed via Συνδρομές).
12. Settings → past-dues import from 1/1 of this year: the count matches; running it again books 0.
13. Αναφορές: totals per category, per account, VAT per rate; CSV opens in Excel with Greek text intact and `;` as separator (columns split correctly); the print view hides the sidebar and prints cleanly.
14. With no AI key at all: the scan button is hidden and the page says why; manual entry still works.
15. Settings → AI → connection test for the vision models: Groq (`meta-llama/llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct`) and Grok (`grok-4-fast-non-reasoning`). These are unverified live — confirm each answers an image request, and that a scan falls through to the next provider when one fails. DeepSeek is never used for scanning.
16. `php cron_finance_due.php` with a document due today: a notification «Τιμολόγιο λήγει σήμερα» reaches the system admins and users with the «Οικονομικά» permission; a second run sends nothing. The reminder is recorded only once delivered to at least one person.
17. Hostinger: the cron job runs `cron_daily.php` (which calls `cron_finance_due.php`) — confirm in hPanel and after a day check the notification.
18. Amounts (el-GR): in Κινήσεις → Κίνηση χωρίς παραστατικό type «1.500» (a 1.500 € grant): the list shows 1.500,00 €, not 1,50 €. Also «1.234,56», «12,40», «1500.00», «12.5». «1.23.4» and «abc» are refused with «Μη έγκυρο ποσό …». In a document, a VAT cell «1.234,56» is kept (the row is not dropped) and a bad cell refuses the save with the typed values kept. Settings → opening balance «2.000» gives 2.000,00 €.
19. Backlog entry date: on 23/11 enter a receipt dated 12/10 with «Πληρώθηκε ήδη» on: the payment is dated 12/10 (cash book and the October card). Change the document date: the payment date follows; type a different payment date by hand: it stops following. A scan that fills the date moves the payment date too. In the «Πληρωμές» box of an existing document the date defaults to today.
20. Books start: set «Έναρξη βιβλίων» = 1/1 this year in Ρυθμίσεις. In Ετήσιες Συνδρομές edit the receipt number of a dues row paid last year: no movement appears in Κινήσεις and the cash balance does not change; a row paid after 1/1 still books. Clear the setting: the earliest opening date of an active account guards instead. The first past-dues import sets the setting when it is empty.
21. Dues method: edit a dues row, the «Τρόπος» field is a list (Μετρητά, Τραπεζική κατάθεση, Κάρτα, IRIS, Άλλο); an old free-text value shows as «… (παλιά τιμή)» and stays selected. Saving «Τραπεζική κατάθεση» keeps the 30 € in the bank account, never moves it to cash.
22. Direction toggle: on Νέο έξοδο scan a donation slip: the note mentions the Έσοδο / Έξοδο switch. Flip it to Έσοδο: the badge, the «Από (δωρητής / πληρωτής)» label, «Εισπράχθηκε ήδη» and the category list change; typed values, the file and the scan stay; the saved document is an income. A document with a payment shows no switch; an unpaid one does, and switching it saves.
23. Due date and «Πληρώθηκε ήδη»: on a new document set a due date by hand (and, separately, by a scan that returns one): the switch unticks once and the note «Με προθεσμία πληρωμής: σημειώθηκε ως απλήρωτο. Τσεκάρετε αν έχει ήδη πληρωθεί.» shows. Re-tick it: it stays ticked and the note goes away.
24. Reminders: three stages in Greek, one per day boundary: «Τιμολόγιο λήγει σύντομα» (7 days before), «Τιμολόγιο λήγει σήμερα», «Ληγμένο τιμολόγιο» (after the due date); each is sent once.
25. Privacy and timeout: the notice under the file box names the provider(s) and says free plans may keep the image under the provider terms and not to scan personal data of private individuals. Scan with a slow/failing first provider (e.g. a wrong Gemini key): the chain moves on and the whole attempt ends within about 75 s with a Greek message; the status line says «έως ~1 λεπτό».
26. Round two: a fresh install with no opening dates shows the amber «Ορίστε “Έναρξη βιβλίων”…» warning on Οικονομικά, Ρυθμίσεις and (finance managers) Ετήσιες Συνδρομές; a non-zero opening balance without a date is refused; saving the first opening date fills «Έναρξη βιβλίων». Editing a booked dues row to a date before the start deletes its movement and the flash says «(Δεν καταχωρήθηκε στο ταμείο…)». «1.500 €» and «€ 12,40» are accepted. Saving a movement shows «Η κίνηση καταχωρήθηκε: 1.500,00 €.». On an income document a due date gives «ανείσπρακτο … εισπραχθεί». Pay date: pick a date via the picker only (change event) and it stops following the document date.
