[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE).

The AGPL's central obligation is **section 13**: if you modify VolunteerOps and
let other people use it over a network, you must offer those users the complete
corresponding source code of your modified version, under the AGPL.

In practice this is a good fit if you are:

- a volunteer organisation running VolunteerOps for your own members
- a public authority deploying it internally

Running an unmodified copy for your own organisation creates no publication
obligation at all.

## Option 2 — Commercial licence (paid)

A commercial licence removes the AGPL obligations. You would want one if you
intend to:

- offer VolunteerOps, or a modified version, as a hosted or managed service to
  third parties without publishing your changes
- integrate VolunteerOps into a proprietary product or platform
- deploy a customised, branded version whose modifications must stay private
- resell deployments to municipalities, agencies or other organisations
- obtain warranty, indemnity, an SLA or contractual support — none of which the
  AGPL provides

Commercial licences are available per organisation and typically include
installation, branding, migration from your existing system, training and
priority support.

If you are not comfortable with that, please open an issue to discuss before
